CameraStack Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 20, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026
Core Principle: CameraStack is engineered with a strict reliability-first, privacy-first architecture. No camera photos, videos, or audio recordings ever leave your local device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how CameraStack ("the Application") handles user data and diagnostics. We believe in complete transparency regarding the diagnostic and performance information necessary to deliver stable, high-performance camera functionality across diverse Android hardware.

2. Data We DO NOT Collect Strict Protection

  • Photos & Videos: Media captured using the app is stored strictly in your device's local media storage or private app directory. No photos or video files are uploaded to any external server.
  • Audio Recordings: The microphone is utilized solely during video capture to encode audio into your local video file. No audio data is uploaded, streamed, or processed off-device.
  • Biometrics & Faces: Any on-device frame analysis (such as QR decoding or focus tracking) occurs exclusively in local volatile memory. No biometric data or facial embeddings are generated or exported.
  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, accounts, or precise GPS locations.

3. Data We Collect Diagnostic Only

To identify device-specific hardware bugs (quirks), validate preview frame rates, and measure camera initialization latency across Android OEMs, we collect anonymized technical metadata:

  • Device Specifications: Manufacturer, model, Android API level, RAM tier, and system-on-chip (SoC) architecture.
  • Camera Performance Metrics: Preview first-frame latency, photo capture completion time, frame-per-second (FPS) stability, and analyzer throughput.
  • Session Diagnostic State: Selected backend engine (CameraX vs. Camera2), hardware capability level, session token, and feature flag states.
  • Crash & Error Reports: Anonymized stack traces and error failure codes (e.g., camera disconnection or buffer stall signatures).

4. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

In accordance with our strict data retention windows, diagnostic telemetry is purged according to the following schedule:

Data Category Retention Period Purpose & Scope
Local Session Telemetry 30 Days Automatically deleted on-device after 30 days via local rolling purge.
Crash Signatures 90 Days De-identified failure clusters used for long-term stability fixes.
Aggregated Metrics 180 Days Device-bucket P95 latency benchmarks for version comparisons.

Immediate Deletion: Because CameraStack does not require user accounts, uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage from Android Settings immediately and permanently purges all local diagnostic data.

5. User Choice & Control

You have full control over diagnostic telemetry. You can opt out at any time directly within the application by navigating to:

Settings > Developer / Diagnostics > Diagnostic Data > Toggle Off

When toggled off, no diagnostic events or telemetry logs are generated or recorded.

6. Security of Data

When diagnostic summaries are transmitted for crash investigation, all data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption. App backup extraction of telemetry is explicitly disabled in the application manifest to prevent extraction via ADB or cloud backups.
